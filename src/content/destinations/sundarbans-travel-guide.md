The Sundarbans is a UNESCO World Heritage site and the largest contiguous mangrove forest on Earth. Spanning southern Bangladesh and India across the Ganges-Brahmaputra delta, it is home to spotted deer, saltwater crocodiles, diverse birdlife, and the Royal Bengal Tiger. Planning your boat safari during the cool months outlined in our [Best Time to Visit Guide](/plan-your-trip/best-time-to-visit/) ensures comfortable river conditions.

## How to Visit & Safari Permits

The Sundarbans cannot be explored on foot or via standard roads; all travel takes place by motorized river vessels equipped for multi-day navigation.

### Permitted Wildlife Zones & Safety
* **Katamka & Kachikhali:** Coastal wildlife sanctuaries featuring forest watchtowers and beach estuaries.
* **Permit & Guard Rules:** All foreign visitors entering the Sundarbans Reserved Forest must obtain official entry permits issued by the Forest Department. Licensed tour operators arrange mandatory permits and assign official armed forest guards to accompany every boat expedition. For general travel precautions, read our [Bangladesh Travel Safety Guide](/bangladesh-travel-safety/).

## Gateway Hubs & Mobile Connectivity
* **Mongla / Khulna:** Primary departure ports where river safari vessels embark for multi-day river voyages. Read our [Transport in Bangladesh Guide](/plan-your-trip/transport-in-bangladesh/) for options on traveling from Dhaka to Khulna.
* **Mobile Signal:** Cellular signal inside deep forest waterways is limited. Grameenphone offers the strongest coastal network coverage; review our [SIM Cards and eSIMs Guide](/plan-your-trip/sim-cards-and-esims/) prior to departure.
