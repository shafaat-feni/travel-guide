**Chattogram**, also widely known internationally by its former English name **Chittagong**, is a major port city in southeastern Bangladesh. It is an important commercial centre with a long maritime history, busy markets, religious landmarks, museums and pockets of green space among its built-up neighbourhoods.

Travellers may see either spelling on airline tickets, booking platforms, railway information and older travel references. When searching for transport or accommodation, try both **Chattogram** and **Chittagong** to find the widest range of results. This guide focuses on the city itself: its urban attractions, local transport, accommodation and practical planning. It does not cover nearby regions as separate destinations.

## Best Things to Do in Chattogram

### 1. Visit the Chattogram Ethnological Museum

Located in the Agrabad area, the Ethnological Museum introduces visitors to the cultures, traditions and ways of life of different communities in Bangladesh. It is a useful stop for travellers who want context for the country's cultural diversity rather than only visiting outdoor attractions.

Check opening days, hours, entry arrangements and photography rules before visiting, as these details can change. Allow time to reach Agrabad in city traffic, and check whether the museum will be open on your planned day.

### 2. Pay your respects at Chattogram War Cemetery

The Chattogram War Cemetery commemorates Commonwealth service personnel who died during the Second World War and is maintained as a place of remembrance. The cemetery is quiet and carefully kept; visitors should behave respectfully, follow posted instructions and avoid disturbing people who have come to pay their respects.

Check visitor access and opening arrangements with the relevant cemetery authority before travelling. This is a memorial site, not an entertainment attraction, so plan a calm, considerate visit.

### 3. Spend time at Foy's Lake

Foy's Lake is a man-made lake in the city's hilly northern area. The lake and its surroundings are used for leisure, and a privately managed amusement park operates in the wider area. It can suit visitors looking for a break from museums and city streets.

Confirm the current entry arrangements, operating hours and separate charges for activities before setting out. If you are visiting mainly for the scenery, check what areas are accessible without purchasing amusement-park activities.

### 4. See the city from Batali Hill

Batali Hill, in the Tigerpass area, is known as a city viewpoint. The climb and surrounding roads may be steep, so wear shoes with good grip and avoid attempting the walk in heavy rain or during the hottest part of the day. Views can be affected by haze, weather and urban development.

Check local access and conditions before going, and do not enter restricted land or climb unsafe slopes. If you visit near sunset, arrange your return transport in advance rather than relying on finding a vehicle immediately.

### 5. Explore the historic city centre around Laldighi and the Court Building

Laldighi and the historic Court Building area offer a glimpse of Chattogram's older civic centre and colonial-era urban history. This is best approached as a short, self-guided walk through a busy part of the city rather than a large, formal monument complex.

Government offices and nearby streets remain working urban spaces. Be considerate around official buildings, check signs about photography and access, and avoid blocking entrances. Traffic and pedestrian conditions can be challenging, so allow extra time to cross roads safely.

### 6. Visit the Bayazid Bostami Shrine

The shrine associated with Bayazid Bostami is an important religious site in Chattogram. Visitors should dress modestly, speak quietly, follow instructions from caretakers and ask before taking photographs of worshippers or restricted areas. Access to particular spaces may depend on local rules and religious observances.

### 7. Visit Patenga Sea Beach for an urban waterfront outing

Patenga is a well-known waterfront area at the southern edge of Chattogram, near the mouth of the Karnaphuli River and the Bay of Bengal. Many visitors come in the late afternoon for the sea air and views. The area can become crowded, particularly on weekends and public holidays.

This is an urban waterfront outing rather than a remote beach escape. Check weather and local conditions, keep valuables secure in crowded areas, follow safety signs and do not enter the water where conditions or warnings make swimming unsafe. Food stalls and facilities vary, so carry essentials you may need.

### 8. Learn about the port city's maritime role

Chattogram's identity is closely tied to its port and the Karnaphuli River. The port is a working industrial and shipping area, not a general visitor attraction, and access to operational facilities is restricted. Enjoy public views only from lawful, accessible locations; do not enter port property, photograph restricted infrastructure or assume that a commercial port tour is available.

## How Long to Stay

- **One day:** Choose two or three city attractions in the same general area, then visit a waterfront area if time and traffic allow.
- **Two days:** A more comfortable stay for combining museums, historic landmarks, Foy's Lake or Batali Hill, and Patenga without rushing between distant parts of the city.
- **Three days:** Suitable for travellers who prefer a slower pace, have business or family visits in the city, or want flexibility for weather and traffic.

Chattogram is spread out, and journey times can be unpredictable. Group attractions geographically instead of trying to cross the city repeatedly in one day.

## Best Time to Visit Chattogram

**November to February** is generally a comfortable period for city sightseeing, with cooler conditions than the hotter months. This is a popular travel season, so reserve accommodation early if your dates coincide with public holidays or major events.

The monsoon season brings heavier rain and can affect road journeys, outdoor viewpoints and waterfront plans. Heat and humidity can make walking tiring at other times of year. Check the forecast shortly before travel and keep outdoor activities flexible.

For a broader seasonal overview, see [Best Time to Visit Bangladesh](/plan-your-trip/best-time-to-visit/).

## How to Get to Chattogram

### By air

Shah Amanat International Airport serves Chattogram, with domestic connections including Dhaka and selected international services depending on the current airline schedule. Flight routes and frequencies can change, so check the airline or booking platform for your dates. Search under both “Chattogram” and “Chittagong” if a platform does not return the expected results.

Before departure, review our [Dhaka Airport Arrival Guide](/plan-your-trip/dhaka-airport-arrival-guide/) if your journey connects through Dhaka, and check your airline's baggage and connection rules.

### By train

Chattogram is connected to Dhaka and other destinations by rail. Journey times, train names, operating days, classes and ticket availability vary, so check the official [Bangladesh Railway website](https://railway.gov.bd/){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} and its linked ticketing service before booking. Use the spelling accepted by the booking system, and buy tickets through official channels.

If you are travelling on a tight schedule, leave a buffer between your train's expected arrival and any onward flight or important appointment.

### By bus or private car

Intercity buses and private vehicles connect Chattogram with other parts of Bangladesh. Travel time depends on the route, traffic, weather, roadworks and departure terminal. Confirm the operator's departure point and arrival point before booking: a city's bus terminals may be far from your hotel or next connection.

For general advice on transport options and booking, see [Transport in Bangladesh](/plan-your-trip/transport-in-bangladesh/).

## Getting Around Chattogram

Local travel options include CNG auto-rickshaws, app-based rides where available, taxis and city buses. Availability, fares and journey times can vary by neighbourhood and time of day.

- Agree on the fare and destination before starting a trip in a vehicle without a metered fare.
- For app-based rides, verify the vehicle and driver details before getting in.
- Allow extra time during commuter periods and around busy commercial areas.
- For several sightseeing stops, compare the cost of separate rides with arranging a vehicle and waiting time in advance.
- Avoid assuming that two attractions close together on a map are a quick walk apart; road layouts, crossings and hills can make a short distance inconvenient.

Keep your accommodation address saved offline and, if possible, written in a form that a local driver can recognise. See [Money and Currency in Bangladesh](/plan-your-trip/money-and-currency/) for practical payment advice.

## Where to Stay in Chattogram

The best area depends on the purpose of your visit. Staying near a central commercial area can be convenient for restaurants, shopping and business appointments, while accommodation closer to your planned attractions may reduce travel time. Properties near major roads can be easier to reach but may be noisy.

Before booking, check the exact address on a map, recent guest reviews, cancellation terms, whether breakfast is included, and how long it usually takes to reach the places you plan to visit. If you arrive late, confirm reception hours and arrange airport or station transport in advance.

Do not choose a hotel based only on its advertised distance from the city centre. Chattogram's traffic and road network can make actual journey time more useful than straight-line distance.

## Food and Shopping

Chattogram has a broad range of restaurants, local eateries, bakeries and markets. If you want to try unfamiliar dishes, ask about ingredients and spice levels, particularly if you have allergies or dietary requirements. Choose food vendors with good turnover and handle drinking water carefully.

Markets and shopping areas can be busy. Keep wallets and phones secure, check prices before agreeing to a purchase or service, and use reputable shops for items where quality or authenticity matters.

For practical advice on dishes, ordering and dietary choices, see our [Bangladesh Food Guide](/experiences/bangladesh-food-guide/) if available. For general customs and respectful behaviour, read [Cultural Etiquette in Bangladesh](/plan-your-trip/cultural-etiquette/).

## Practical Tips for International Visitors

- **Carry some cash:** Small shops, street vendors and some local transport providers may not accept cards. Do not carry more cash than you need for the day.
- **Plan around traffic:** Put nearby attractions together and leave a buffer for airport, railway station and bus-terminal journeys.
- **Dress appropriately:** Lightweight clothing is useful in warm weather. Dress modestly when visiting religious sites and memorials.
- **Protect yourself from heat and rain:** Carry drinking water, sun protection and a compact umbrella or rain jacket as appropriate for the season.
- **Keep valuables secure:** Use extra care in crowded markets, terminals and waterfront areas.
- **Confirm opening arrangements:** Museum hours, entry conditions and attraction facilities can change. Check directly before making a special journey.
- **Respect restricted areas:** The port and other operational facilities are not automatically open to visitors. Follow signs and instructions.
- **Keep plans flexible:** Heavy rain, traffic and public events can disrupt an otherwise straightforward day.

## Suggested One-Day Chattogram Itinerary

**Morning:** Start with the Ethnological Museum if it is open on your chosen day. Check visiting hours in advance and allow time for the journey to Agrabad.

**Late morning or midday:** Choose a nearby meal stop, then visit one historic city-centre landmark such as the Laldighi and Court Building area, respecting access rules around working government offices.

**Afternoon:** Visit Foy's Lake or Batali Hill, depending on your interests, weather and local access. Avoid trying to fit both in if traffic is heavy.

**Late afternoon:** If conditions and travel time allow, continue to Patenga for the waterfront atmosphere. Allow enough time to return to your accommodation.

This is a flexible outline, not a guarantee that all sites will be open or reachable within the same day. Check conditions locally and remove a stop rather than rushing across the city.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Chattogram the same as Chattogram?

Yes. Chattogram is the city's official English name, while Chattogram remains widely used by international services, older references and some booking platforms. Try both spellings when searching for transport or accommodation.

### Is Chattogram worth visiting for tourists?

It can be worthwhile for visitors interested in urban history, museums, religious landmarks, local food and the life of a major port city. It is also a commercial hub, so the experience is more urban and busy than a dedicated nature retreat.

### How many days should I spend in Chattogram?

One full day can cover a few selected attractions, but two days allow a more relaxed visit. The city is spread out, and traffic can make it difficult to visit many places in a short time.

### Can visitors tour Chattogram Port?

The port is an operational shipping facility, and access to restricted areas is not generally available to casual visitors. Do not enter port property without official authorisation; use only lawful public viewpoints.

### What is the easiest way to get around the city?

CNG auto-rickshaws, app-based rides where available, taxis and buses can all be useful. The best option depends on distance, traffic, time of day and whether you are making several stops. Confirm fares or ride details before travelling.

### What should I check before visiting the city's attractions?

Confirm opening days, hours, entry arrangements, photography rules and access conditions directly before you go. These details may change, and not every attraction is open every day.
