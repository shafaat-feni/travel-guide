Dhaka, the bustling capital of Bangladesh, is a dynamic megacity where 400-year-old Mughal architecture sits alongside modern elevated metro lines. For foreign tourists arriving at [Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC)](/plan-your-trip/dhaka-airport-arrival-guide/), Dhaka offers a rich cultural experience characterized by vibrant street markets, historic riverfronts, and legendary culinary heritage.

## Major Attractions in Dhaka

### Lalbagh Fort (Fort Aurangabad)
Built in the 17th century during the Mughal Empire, Lalbagh Fort is an incomplete fortress complex housing the tomb of Pari Bibi, a three-domed mosque, and Mughal gardens.

* **Visiting Hours:** Typically open Tuesday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Monday closed). Entry fees for foreign nationals are payable in cash at the official ticket kiosk (see our [Money & Currency Guide](/plan-your-trip/money-and-currency/) for payment customs).
* **Location:** Lalbagh, Old Dhaka.

### Ahsan Manzil (The Pink Palace)
Located along the banks of the Buriganga River, Ahsan Manzil was the official seat and palace of the Nawab of Dhaka. Today, it serves as a designated national museum exhibiting 19th-century artifacts. When visiting historic sites, review our guide to [Cultural Etiquette in Bangladesh](/plan-your-trip/cultural-etiquette/) for conservative dress guidelines.

## Transport & Getting Around

Navigating Dhaka requires selecting the right transit mode depending on distance and traffic conditions:
* **Dhaka Metro Rail (MRT Line 6):** The elevated mass transit system connects northern Dhaka (Uttara) through Farmgate to Motijheel, avoiding surface traffic bottlenecks.
* **App-Based Rideshares:** Uber operates widely throughout Dhaka for both cars and motorbikes, providing upfront fares.
* **Traditional Cycle Rickshaws:** Ideal for short distance travel within neighborhood zones like Gulshan, Banani, or Old Dhaka's narrow alleys. Always negotiate the fare before boarding.
