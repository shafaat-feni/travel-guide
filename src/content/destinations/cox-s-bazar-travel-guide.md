Stretching uninterrupted for over 120 kilometers along the Bay of Bengal, Cox's Bazar is Bangladesh's premier coastal beach resort destination. Planning your visit during the [Best Time to Visit Bangladesh](/plan-your-trip/best-time-to-visit/) (November through March) ensures dry weather, comfortable sea breezes, and clear water.

## Highlights & Coastal Beaches

### Laboni & Kolatoli Beach
The main tourist beach areas adjacent to the central hotel district. Lifeguard towers and tourist police posts are present along major entry access points. For security guidance, consult our [Bangladesh Travel Safety Guide](/bangladesh-travel-safety/).

### Inani Beach & Marine Drive
Located 30 kilometers south of central Cox's Bazar, Inani Beach is famous for its coral rocks and quiet shoreline. The journey via the scenic Marine Drive offers stunning coastal views between green hills and the ocean.

## How to Get to Cox's Bazar

* **By Air:** Cox's Bazar Airport (CXB) handles multiple daily 60-minute domestic flights departing from [Dhaka's Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC)](/plan-your-trip/dhaka-airport-arrival-guide/) via airlines such as Biman Bangladesh and US-Bangla.
* **By Train:** Direct intercity rail service connects Dhaka Railway Station (Kamalapur) directly to the modern shell-shaped Cox's Bazar Railway Station. Review our complete [Transport in Bangladesh Guide](/plan-your-trip/transport-in-bangladesh/) for train e-ticketing steps.
