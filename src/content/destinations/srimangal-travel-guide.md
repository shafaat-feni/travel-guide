Srimangal (Sreemangal) is recognized as the tea capital of Bangladesh. Surrounded by rolling green hills blanketed in tea gardens, rubber plantations, and lemon groves, it is one of the quietest eco-tourism destinations in the country, easily reached from nearby [Sylhet](/destinations/sylhet-travel-guide/).

## Top Sights & Experiences

### Lawachara National Park
A tropical rainforest sanctuary home to the endangered Western Hoolock Gibbon. Well-marked hiking trails ranging from 30 minutes to 3 hours allow visitors to trek under dense forest canopy accompanied by licensed forest guides. When trekking outdoors, follow mosquito prevention advice in our [Travel Health Guide](/bangladesh-travel-health/).

### Finlay & Zareen Tea Estates
Walk or cycle through lush terraced tea estates. Visit local tea tasting shops to sample regional tea blends, including Srimangal's famous multi-layered tea. Review seasonal weather patterns in our [Best Time to Visit Guide](/plan-your-trip/best-time-to-visit/).

## How to Arrive
* **By Train:** Srimangal is a primary stop on the Dhaka-Sylhet main railway line (approx. 4.5 hours from Dhaka). Check our [Transport in Bangladesh Guide](/plan-your-trip/transport-in-bangladesh/) for train schedules and Snigdha AC chair class details.
