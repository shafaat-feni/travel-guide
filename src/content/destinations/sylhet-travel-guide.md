Nestled in northeastern Bangladesh near the Meghalaya border, Sylhet is famous for its rolling tea plantations, tropical wetland ecosystems, and spiritual heritage. Visiting during the cool months outlined in our [Best Time to Visit Guide](/plan-your-trip/best-time-to-visit/) offers ideal weather for outdoor exploration.

## Key Attractions

### Hazrat Shah Jalal Shrine
A deeply respected historical and spiritual site located in central Sylhet, honoring the 14th-century Sufi scholar Hazrat Shah Jalal. Visitors are welcomed while maintaining quiet etiquette and respectful attire. Read our guide to [Cultural Etiquette in Bangladesh](/plan-your-trip/cultural-etiquette/) before visiting active religious shrines.

### Ratargul Swamp Forest & Jaflong
Ratargul is one of South Asia's few freshwater swamp forests, best explored by small wooden boats during the late monsoon. Further east along the Indian border lies Jaflong, known for crystal-clear stream waters flowing from the Meghalaya hills.

## Getting There & Around
* **Flights & Trains:** Osmani International Airport (ZYL) in Sylhet receives daily domestic flights from Dhaka, while daily intercity trains depart from Dhaka's Kamalapur station. Consult our [Transport in Bangladesh Guide](/plan-your-trip/transport-in-bangladesh/) for booking advice.
* **Nearby Regions:** Combine your Sylhet itinerary with neighboring [Srimangal](/destinations/srimangal-travel-guide/), the tea capital of Bangladesh located just 2 hours south by train.
