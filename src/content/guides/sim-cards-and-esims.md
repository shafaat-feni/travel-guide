Staying connected to mobile internet is essential for navigating Bangladesh, using rideshare apps like Uber, and accessing real-time travel information. Foreign tourists can easily acquire local prepaid SIM cards or configure international travel eSIMs upon arrival.

## Mobile Network Operators in Bangladesh

Bangladesh features four primary telecommunications providers regulated by the Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission (BTRC):

1. **Grameenphone (GP):** The largest network operator in Bangladesh, offering the widest 4G coverage in rural areas, national parks, hill districts, and coastal regions.
2. **Banglalink:** A major national carrier offering competitive data packages and strong urban signal coverage.
3. **Robi Axiata:** Known for excellent urban coverage and fast mobile data speeds in major cities like [Dhaka](/destinations/dhaka-travel-guide/), Chattogram, and [Sylhet](/destinations/sylhet-travel-guide/).
4. **Teletalk:** The state-owned network operator.

For foreign tourists traveling beyond major cities to destinations like the [Sundarbans](/destinations/sundarbans-travel-guide/), [Srimangal](/destinations/srimangal-travel-guide/), or the Chittagong Hill Tracts, **Grameenphone** is generally recommended for consistent signal coverage.

## Passport Registration Rules for Foreign Tourists

Under BTRC regulations, all mobile SIM cards issued in Bangladesh require mandatory identity registration linked to the user's passport and entry visa details.

### Documents Required at the Shop:
* **Original Passport:** Must have at least 6 months remaining validity.
* **Valid Entry Visa / Entry Stamp:** Present your stamped visa or Visa on Arrival (VoA) entry stamp received at immigration (see our [Tourist Visa Requirements Guide](/plan-your-trip/bangladesh-visa/)).
* **Biometric Verification:** The authorized representative will record a digital scan of your index fingerprints as mandated by BTRC rules.
* **Active Connection Period:** Tourist SIM connections issued against a foreign passport remain active for the validity period authorized during registration. If you extend your stay, visit an official operator customer care center (*Grameenphone Center*, *Robi Sheba*, etc.) with your passport to update your registration.

> **Safety Warning:** Never purchase unregistered SIM cards from unofficial street vendors. Connections registered under another individual's identity violate telecommunication regulations and risk immediate deactivation. Always buy directly from official operator stalls.

## Purchasing a SIM Card at Dhaka Airport

The most convenient location to purchase a local SIM card is inside the arrival concourse at [Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC)](/plan-your-trip/dhaka-airport-arrival-guide/) in Dhaka.

* **Location:** Official Grameenphone and Banglalink desks are positioned in the exit hall after clearing Customs near Exit Green Channel 01.
* **Payment:** Cash in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT) is required. Exchange currency or withdraw cash from airport ATMs (see our [Money and Currency Guide](/plan-your-trip/money-and-currency/)) before visiting the SIM counter.
* **Activation Time:** Tourist SIM cards registered at official airport counters are typically activated within 15 to 30 minutes.

## Using International eSIMs in Bangladesh

If your smartphone supports eSIM technology, purchasing a digital travel plan prior to arrival allows instant connectivity without visiting a local shop or swapping physical SIM cards.

* **eSIM Options:** Global travel eSIM providers (such as Airalo, Maya Mobile, or Nomad) offer digital data plans operating on local Bangladeshi networks.
* **Considerations:** While eSIMs provide convenience and immediate connectivity upon landing, international data packages are typically more expensive per gigabyte than purchasing a physical local tourist SIM card on-site.
