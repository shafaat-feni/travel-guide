Planning the timing of your trip to Bangladesh is critical to ensuring comfortable weather and optimal conditions for wildlife safaris, beach travel, and sightseeing. Bangladesh experiences a tropical monsoon climate characterized by three main seasons: the cool dry season, the hot summer season, and the southwest monsoon season.

## Seasonal Breakdown

### 1. Cool Dry Season (November to February) — Peak Tourist Season
* **Temperature:** Highs of 20°C to 28°C (68°F–82°F); cool evenings down to 10°C–15°C (50°F–59°F).
* **Conditions:** Low humidity, minimal rainfall, and clear skies.
* **Best For:** Overall sightseeing, hiking in Srimangal tea estates, beach trips to [Cox's Bazar](/destinations/cox-s-bazar-travel-guide/), and boat safaris in the [Sundarbans](/destinations/sundarbans-travel-guide/).
* **Planning Tip:** This is the peak travel window. Book intercity [trains](/plan-your-trip/transport-in-bangladesh/) and major hotels in advance.

### 2. Hot Summer Season (March to May)
* **Temperature:** Highs ranging from 32°C to 38°C (90°F–100°F).
* **Conditions:** High heat, rising humidity, and occasional brief afternoon thunderstorms (locally known as *Kalbaishakhi* or Nor'westers).
* **Best For:** Exploring northeastern tea plantations in [Sylhet](/destinations/sylhet-travel-guide/) and avoiding winter tourist crowds. Carry lightweight, breathable clothing and sun protection.

### 3. Monsoon Season (June to October)
* **Temperature:** Highs of 28°C to 32°C (82°F–90°F) with high humidity.
* **Conditions:** Heavy seasonal rainfall across the country, swelling rivers and flooding low-lying delta areas.
* **Best For:** Experiencing lush green landscapes, full river waterfalls in Jaflong, and flooded swamp forest boat trips in [Sylhet](/destinations/sylhet-travel-guide/).
* **Planning Tip:** Check weather updates for coastal maritime warnings and review our [Travel Health Guide](/bangladesh-travel-health/) regarding mosquito precautions during monsoon months.
