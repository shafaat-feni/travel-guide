Navigating transport in Bangladesh is an essential part of the travel experience. From modern elevated metro lines and domestic flights to historic intercity railways and river passenger launches, choosing the right transport mode ensures a comfortable journey.

## Intercity Trains (Bangladesh Railway)

Bangladesh Railway operates an extensive network connecting major regional hubs, including [Dhaka](/destinations/dhaka-travel-guide/), Chattogram, [Sylhet](/destinations/sylhet-travel-guide/), [Srimangal](/destinations/srimangal-travel-guide/), Rajshahi, and [Cox's Bazar](/destinations/cox-s-bazar-travel-guide/).

### Recommended Train Classes
* **Snigdha (AC Chair):** Air-conditioned seating cars offering comfortable recliners and dedicated luggage space.
* **AC Seat / AC Berth:** Available on long-distance sleeper services, providing privacy for overnight journeys.

### Ticket Booking Advice for Foreign Tourists
* **Online Booking Portal:** The official <a href="https://eticket.railway.gov.bd/" target="blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bangladesh Railway E-Ticketing Portal</a> opens tickets for purchase up to 10 days prior to departure. However, registering an online user account requires a valid Bangladeshi mobile number for verification.
* **Station Ticket Counters:** Foreign tourists who cannot complete online registration should visit the main reservation counters at major railway stations (such as Kamalapur Railway Station in Dhaka) in person with their physical passport to purchase seats from station quotas.

## Domestic Flights

Domestic air travel is the fastest option for covering long distances within Bangladesh, reducing 8-hour road trips to 50-minute flights.

* **Primary Airlines:** Biman Bangladesh Airlines (national flag carrier), US-Bangla Airlines, and Air Astra.
* **Key Domestic Routes:** Flights depart daily from [Dhaka's Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC)](/plan-your-trip/dhaka-airport-arrival-guide/) to [Cox's Bazar (CXB)](/destinations/cox-s-bazar-travel-guide/), [Sylhet (ZYL)](/destinations/sylhet-travel-guide/), Chattogram (CGP), Saidpur (SPD), and Barishal (BZL).

## River Transport & Launches

Bangladesh is defined by its vast river network formed by the Ganges, Brahmaputra, and Meghna rivers. Traveling by water offers scenic views of delta river life.

* **Passenger Launches:** Large multi-deck river vessels operate south from Dhaka's Sadarghat Launch Terminal to southern river delta districts such as Barishal and Bhola.
* **VIP Cabins:** For overnight river travel, booking a private air-conditioned VIP cabin is strongly recommended for comfort and security.

## Urban City Transport & Rideshares

* **Dhaka Metro Rail (MRT Line 6):** The fastest way to bypass surface traffic in [Dhaka](/destinations/dhaka-travel-guide/). Modern, clean, air-conditioned trains connect northern areas (Uttara) through Farmgate to Motijheel.
* **App-Based Rideshares:** Uber operates extensively in major metropolitan areas, offering transparent, pre-negotiated pricing for cars and motorbikes.
* **Cycle Rickshaws:** Traditional three-wheeled rickshaws are ubiquitous for short neighborhood trips. Always agree on the fare before starting your journey (see cash tips in our [Money and Currency Guide](/plan-your-trip/money-and-currency/)).
