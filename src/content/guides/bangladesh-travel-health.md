Taking sensible health precautions ensures a comfortable and safe journey through Bangladesh. Consulting a travel medicine clinic or your primary physician 4 to 6 weeks before departure is recommended to ensure you receive appropriate immunizations and preventative advice tailored to your health history.

## Recommended Vaccinations

Public health agencies (such as the WHO and U.S. CDC) recommend the following standard vaccines for travelers visiting Bangladesh:

* **Routine Vaccines:** Ensure routine immunizations (Tetanus, Diphtheria, Pertussis, Measles, Mumps, Rubella, and Polio) are up to date.
* **Hepatitis A:** Recommended for all unvaccinated travelers to guard against contaminated food and water.
* **Typhoid:** Recommended for most international travelers, particularly those visiting smaller towns, rural districts, or eating street food.
* **Hepatitis B & Rabies:** Recommended for long-term travelers, healthcare workers, or outdoor adventurers in rural environments.

## Food and Water Safety

Digestive upset ("traveler's diarrhea") is a common issue for foreign visitors unaccustomed to local water bacteria.

### Safe Water Guidelines:
* **Drink Bottled Water Only:** Use sealed, commercially bottled water for drinking, brushing teeth, and making ice. Check that the factory seal is intact before opening.
* **Avoid Tap Water:** Do not consume untreated tap water or accept drinks containing unverified ice cubes in informal settings.

### Food Hygiene Precautions:
* Eat freshly prepared meals served hot.
* Enjoy street food at busy, high-turnover stalls where food is cooked to order right in front of you.
* Peel fresh fruits yourself before eating, and avoid raw unwashed vegetables in informal eateries.

## Mosquito-Borne Illness Prevention

Bangladesh is a tropical country where mosquito-borne viral infections, particularly **Dengue fever**, occur primarily during and immediately following the monsoon season (June to October).

### Protective Measures:
* Apply insect repellent containing 20%–30% DEET, Picaridin, or Oil of Lemon Eucalyptus to exposed skin.
* Wear loose-fitting, long-sleeved shirts and long trousers during dawn and dusk when mosquitoes are most active.
* Stay in accommodations equipped with air conditioning, window screens, or bed nets.

## Healthcare Facilities & Travel Insurance

* **Private Hospitals in Dhaka:** Excellent private medical facilities with English-speaking physicians operate in Dhaka, including Square Hospital, Evercare Hospital, and United Hospital.
* **Comprehensive Travel Insurance:** Always secure international travel medical insurance that includes coverage for emergency medical treatment and medical evacuation prior to departure.
