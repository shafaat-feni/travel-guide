Bangladesh is known for its legendary hospitality and welcoming population. Most international visits are completed smoothly and without incident. However, maintaining situational awareness, respecting cultural traditions, and adhering to basic safety precautions ensures a safe, enjoyable journey.

## General Security & Situational Awareness

As when traveling in any foreign destination, international visitors should exercise standard security precautions:
* **Crowded Places & Demonstrations:** Avoid political rallies, large public demonstrations, or unannounced street gatherings.
* **Valuables & Petty Theft:** Pickpocketing and bag-snatching can occur in densely populated markets, bus terminals, or crowded railway stations. Keep valuables secure, avoid wearing expensive jewelry, and use crossbody bags.
* **Nighttime Travel:** Minimize walking alone at night in poorly lit or unfamiliar urban areas. Use reputable app-based rideshares (see our [Transport in Bangladesh Guide](/plan-your-trip/transport-in-bangladesh/)) or pre-booked hotel transport after dusk.

## Cultural Norms & Dress Guidelines

Bangladesh is a predominantly conservative society. Respecting local norms fosters positive interactions and minimizes unwanted attention.
* **Modest Attire:** Both men and women are advised to dress conservatively in public areas. Shoulders and knees should remain covered. Consult our complete [Cultural Etiquette Guide](/plan-your-trip/cultural-etiquette/) for detailed norms regarding religious site visits and greetings.
* **Social Etiquette:** Public displays of affection are generally discouraged in public spaces.

Keep these essential emergency numbers recorded in your mobile phone:

| **National Emergency Services** | **999** | Toll-free national dispatch for Police, Fire, and Ambulance |
| **Bangladesh Tourist Police** | **+880 1320-001299** | Dedicated foreign tourist assistance helpline |
| **Dhaka Metropolitan Police** | **100** | General police assistance in [Dhaka metro area](/destinations/dhaka-travel-guide/) |

## Official Travel Advisories & Health Precautions

Foreign governments maintain travel advice portals for their citizens:
* **United States:** Review the [U.S. Department of State Bangladesh Travel Advisory](https://travel.state.gov/).
* **United Kingdom:** Consult the [UK FCDO Foreign Travel Advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/bangladesh).
* **Health Precautions:** Review our [Travel Health & Medical Precautions Guide](/bangladesh-travel-health/) for recommended immunizations and Dengue fever prevention.

Always register your foreign travel with your country's embassy travel registration service (e.g., STEP for U.S. citizens) to receive real-time security alerts during your stay.
