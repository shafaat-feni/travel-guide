Managing your finances smoothly in Bangladesh requires a clear understanding of cash reliance, local currency customs, and payment options. While card processing is widely available at mid-to-high-end hotels and supermarkets in major cities like [Dhaka](/destinations/dhaka-travel-guide/), Bangladesh remains primarily a cash-based economy for daily expenses, street food, cycle rickshaws, and local markets.

## The Local Currency: Bangladeshi Taka (BDT)

The official currency of Bangladesh is the **Bangladeshi Taka** (represented by the symbol **৳** or code **BDT**). 

* **Banknote Denominations:** Banknotes are issued in denominations of ৳1, ৳2, ৳5, ৳10, ৳20, ৳50, ৳100, ৳200, ৳500, and ৳1000.
* **Carrying Small Notes:** Keep a supply of smaller bills (৳20, ৳50, and ৳100) readily accessible. Drivers, rickshaw pullers, and small vendors often struggle to make change for ৳500 or ৳1000 notes.

## Currency Exchange & Declaring Foreign Cash

### Exchanging Foreign Currency
* **Airport Exchange Booths:** Upon arriving at [Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC)](/plan-your-trip/dhaka-airport-arrival-guide/) in Dhaka, authorized commercial bank kiosks in the arrival hall offer currency exchange for major foreign currencies, including US Dollars (USD), Euros (EUR), British Pounds (GBP), and Canadian Dollars (CAD).
* **Official Money Changers:** In urban commercial hubs (such as Gulshan and Motijheel in Dhaka), licensed money exchange booths operate legally under Bangladesh Bank authorization. Always request an official printed receipt.

> **Understanding Foreign Currency Declaration Rules:** 
> * **Bangladesh Bank Central Rule:** Under central bank foreign exchange policy, incoming travelers may bring in foreign currency up to **USD 10,000** (or equivalent) without submitting a formal currency declaration form to Bangladesh Bank.
> * **Customs Baggage Form Question:** However, the official National Board of Revenue (NBR) Customs Baggage Declaration Form administered at airport checkpoints specifically asks incoming passengers whether they are carrying foreign currency **exceeding USD 5,000**.
> * **Practical Travel Advice:** If you are carrying foreign cash between USD 5,000 and USD 10,000, filing a declaration on Form FMJ at the Customs Red Channel upon arrival is strongly recommended. This creates an official receipt that protects you if you wish to take unspent foreign cash back out of the country upon departure.

## Using ATMs and Credit Cards

### International Credit & Debit Cards
* **Card Acceptance:** Visa and Mastercard are accepted at major international hotels, supermarkets (such as Shwapno or Unimart), upscale restaurants, and domestic airline ticketing desks. American Express has limited acceptance outside high-end establishments.
* **Foreign Transaction Notification:** Notify your issuing bank before travel to prevent temporary fraud blocks on your cards while operating in Bangladesh.

### ATM Networks for Foreign Travelers
ATMs (Automated Teller Machines) are widely distributed across major cities, regional towns, and transport hubs.
* **Recommended Bank ATMs:** Standard Chartered Bank, HSBC, Dutch-Bangla Bank Limited (DBBL), Eastern Bank PLC (EBL), and Brac Bank generally support international Visa and Mastercard withdrawals.
* **ATM Fees:** Local bank ATM processing fees may apply in addition to your home bank's foreign exchange charges.

## Tipping Customs & Gratitude

Tipping is appreciated in service industries in Bangladesh, though it is rarely mandatory. For additional context on social etiquette, review our [Cultural Etiquette Guide](/plan-your-trip/cultural-etiquette/):
* **Restaurants:** High-end dining establishments often include a service charge. If no charge is included, leaving a modest tip for attentive service is customary.
* **Tour Guides & Drivers:** Tipping private drivers and licensed tour guides when traveling on [intercity trips](/plan-your-trip/transport-in-bangladesh/) is standard practice for helpful service.
