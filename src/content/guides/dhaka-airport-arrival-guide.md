Arriving at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC) in Dhaka can feel fast-paced for first-time international travelers. Understanding the physical airport layout and terminal flow helps clear immigration, collect baggage, and arrange onward transportation smoothly.

## Step 1: Disembarkation & Arrival Hall Navigation

Upon exiting your aircraft, follow the clear overhead signage for "International Arrivals" and "Immigration." The terminal corridors lead to the central immigration hall located on the upper arrival level.

## Step 2: Immigration Clearance

The arrival immigration area is split into designated counters:
* **Foreign Passport Holders with Advance Visa:** Proceed directly to the general foreign passport clearance lanes. Present your passport, completed embarkation/disembarkation card (distributed onboard or available at desks prior to immigration counters), and return flight details.
* **Visa on Arrival (VoA) Desk:** If you require a Visa on Arrival, visit the dedicated VoA counter prior to joining the main immigration line. Review our detailed [Tourist Visa Requirements Guide](/plan-your-trip/bangladesh-visa/) to confirm eligibility and mandatory documents. Submit your documents, pay the USD fee at the adjacent bank counter, and receive your VoA stamp before proceeding to the immigration officer desk.

## Step 3: Customs Declaration & Baggage Retrieval

After clearing immigration, descend to the baggage claim hall on the ground floor. 
* Locate your flight number on the baggage carousel monitors.
* Once baggage is collected, proceed through Customs. If carrying items above standard allowance, duty-free limits, or major currency exceeding USD 10,000, submit a completed Customs Declaration Form at the Red Channel. Travelers with standard personal luggage proceed through the Green Channel.

## Step 4: Currency Exchange & SIM Cards

Before stepping out of the secure arrival concourse, take advantage of the authorized service kiosks located inside the arrival hall:
* **Currency Exchange Desks:** Licensed commercial bank booths offer currency exchange from USD, EUR, GBP, and CAD into Bangladeshi Taka (BDT). Read our [Money and Currency Guide](/plan-your-trip/money-and-currency/) for details on local exchange rates and foreign ATM card acceptance.
* **Local SIM Card / eSIM Booths:** Major telecommunication providers (such as Grameenphone and Banglalink) maintain official desks. Review our [SIM Cards and eSIMs Guide](/plan-your-trip/sim-cards-and-esims/) for passport registration requirements.

## Step 5: Onward Airport Transportation

Exiting the airport terminal building leads directly to the ground transport pickup area.

### Recommended Transport Options
1. **App-Based Rideshare Services:** Uber operates widely in Dhaka and provides ride pickups directly at designated airport zones. Using app-based rideshares ensures upfront pricing and avoids fare negotiations.
2. **Official Pre-Paid Taxi Desks:** Located inside the arrival hall, official pre-paid taxi service counters issue fixed-rate tickets to major districts in Dhaka (such as Gulshan, Banani, or Dhanmondi; see our [Dhaka Destination Guide](/destinations/dhaka-travel-guide/)).
3. **Dhaka Metro Rail & Trains:** For intercity onward travel, Dhaka Railway Station (Kamalapur) and Metro Line 6 provide rapid transfers. Consult our [Transport in Bangladesh Guide](/plan-your-trip/transport-in-bangladesh/) for train schedule booking options.
