# Dhaka Airport Arrival Guide: Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC)

Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC) is the main international gateway to Dhaka and the airport most international visitors use when flying to Bangladesh. If this is your first visit, knowing what to do after landing can make immigration, baggage collection, customs, and the journey into the city much easier.

This guide explains the practical steps for **arriving at Dhaka airport**, including what to keep in your hand luggage, how to handle a Bangladesh Visa on Arrival if you qualify, where to get local connectivity, and how to choose transport after leaving the terminal. Airport procedures, service counters, and transport arrangements can change, so follow current airport signs and instructions from officials.

## Before you fly to Dhaka: prepare these essentials

Keep the following items in your personal bag, not in checked baggage:

- Your passport and any Bangladesh visa or entry approval required for your trip.
- A printed or offline copy of your return or onward ticket.
- Your hotel booking or the address and contact details of your host.
- Evidence of sufficient funds and any documents needed for a Visa on Arrival application.
- Travel insurance details and emergency contacts.
- A payment card and some cash in an accepted currency for arrival expenses.
- Any required online visa or Visa on Arrival application receipt.
- Your phone, charger, and a way to connect to the internet after landing.

If you have not booked your journey yet, compare routes and fares before purchasing your ticket. Check baggage allowances, transit visa rules, airport changes, and the conditions for changing or cancelling your ticket before paying.

For entry requirements, read our [Bangladesh Tourist Visa Requirements and Visa on Arrival Guide](/plan-your-trip/bangladesh-visa/).

## Step 1: Follow the signs to international arrivals

After leaving the aircraft, follow the airport signs for **Arrivals** and **Immigration**. Keep your passport and visa documents easy to reach. Follow staff instructions if passengers are directed to different corridors or queues.

Do not follow people offering unofficial help with immigration, luggage, currency exchange, or transport. If you need assistance, ask uniformed airport staff or use an official service counter.

## Step 2: Complete immigration clearance

At immigration, present your passport and the visa or entry documents applicable to your situation. Officers may ask about your reason for visiting Bangladesh, where you will stay, how long you intend to remain, or when you plan to leave. Answer clearly and consistently with your documents.

### If you already have a Bangladesh visa

Join the appropriate immigration queue for your passport and visa category. Keep your accommodation details and return or onward ticket available in case the officer asks to see them. Check your entry stamp or electronic record before leaving the counter and raise any apparent error with the officer immediately.

### If you plan to use Visa on Arrival

**Visa on Arrival is not guaranteed and is available only to eligible travelers under the current rules.** Confirm your eligibility before boarding rather than assuming that a particular nationality automatically qualifies.

If you are eligible, follow the current airport signs and instructions for the Visa on Arrival process before joining the main immigration queue. You may need to visit the designated bank counter to pay the fee, then take your receipt and supporting documents to the relevant visa desk. Requirements and procedures can change, and immigration officials make the final decision.

Check the latest eligibility categories and document requirements on the <a href="https://voa.specialbranch.gov.bd/public/voa-info.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">official Bangladesh Special Branch Visa on Arrival information page</a> and its <a href="https://voa.specialbranch.gov.bd/public/voa-instructions.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">official Visa on Arrival instructions</a>. Keep your passport, return ticket, accommodation information, evidence of funds, and any required application receipt ready.

Read the full [Bangladesh Visa Guide](/plan-your-trip/bangladesh-visa/) before travelling. If your eligibility is uncertain, contact the Bangladesh diplomatic mission responsible for you and consider obtaining an advance visa.

## Step 3: Collect your checked baggage

After immigration, follow the signs to baggage reclaim. Check the flight number and carousel information on the monitors rather than relying on a carousel number from another flight.

- Keep your baggage tag until you have left the airport.
- Check your bags before leaving the reclaim area.
- If a bag is missing or damaged, report it to your airline's baggage service desk before leaving the airport and keep the claim reference.
- Do not accept a bag from another traveler or leave your own bags unattended.

If you have only cabin baggage, follow the signs toward customs after completing immigration.

## Step 4: Pass through customs

After collecting your baggage, follow the customs instructions and use the appropriate channel. The **Green Channel is not a general exemption from customs rules**: it is for travelers who have nothing to declare under the applicable regulations. Use the Red Channel or speak to a customs officer if you are carrying goods, cash, or other items that must be declared, or if you are unsure.

Bangladesh customs rules can apply to commercial quantities, restricted or controlled goods, and items beyond permitted allowances. Check current rules before packing medicines, professional equipment, high-value goods, or items you intend to leave in Bangladesh.

### Bringing foreign currency into Bangladesh

Do not rely on a single dollar threshold as a complete description of Bangladesh's currency rules. Declaration requirements can depend on the amount, currency, and circumstances. Check the latest instructions from Bangladesh customs and the relevant financial authorities before travel, and declare currency when required. Keep any declaration receipt securely; it may be needed when taking money out of the country.

For official guidance, consult <a href="https://customs.gov.bd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bangladesh Customs</a>. If you are carrying a substantial amount of cash or have a complex itinerary, verify the rules before departure instead of relying on informal advice.

## Step 5: Exchange money or arrange access to Bangladeshi taka

The local currency is the Bangladeshi taka (BDT). You may find bank or currency-exchange counters in airport public areas, but their locations, opening hours, rates, and available currencies can vary. Check the displayed exchange rate and any commission before completing a transaction, and count your money and collect the receipt at the counter.

You do not need to exchange your entire travel budget at the airport. Consider exchanging enough for immediate expenses—such as transport, a drink, or small purchases—and comparing options later in the city. Keep cash secure and avoid exchanging money with unofficial individuals.

International cards may work at some ATMs and businesses, but acceptance is not universal. Do not assume that every airport desk, taxi, or small business accepts cards. It is sensible to carry a backup payment method.

See our [Money in Bangladesh: Currency, ATMs, Cards and Cash Guide](/plan-your-trip/money-and-currency/) for practical advice on cash withdrawals, card use, exchange rates, and budgeting.

## Step 6: Get a local SIM card or connect with an eSIM

Internet access is useful for maps, ride-hailing apps, contacting your accommodation, and checking onward travel. Depending on your phone and plan, you can use international roaming, a compatible travel eSIM, or a local Bangladeshi SIM.

If you buy a local SIM at the airport, use an official operator outlet or authorized seller and ask what passport, visa, or registration documents are required. Confirm the package price, data allowance, validity period, and whether calls or SMS are included before paying. Keep the SIM packaging or receipt until the service is working.

An eSIM can be convenient if your phone supports it, but check that the plan actually covers Bangladesh and understand whether it provides data only or also a local phone number. Install and configure it using the provider's instructions, ideally while you have reliable internet access.

Read our [SIM Cards and eSIMs in Bangladesh guide](/plan-your-trip/sim-cards-and-esims/) for more details on connectivity, registration, and choosing an option for your stay.

## Step 7: Choose transport from Dhaka airport

Before leaving the terminal, decide how you will reach your hotel or next destination. Your best option depends on arrival time, luggage, budget, traffic, and whether you already have a local phone connection. Dhaka traffic can be heavy, especially during busy periods, so allow a realistic buffer if you have a train, bus, or domestic flight to catch.

### App-based rides

Ride-hailing services may be available in Dhaka, but service availability, pickup points, and airport rules can change. Open your chosen app and follow its current pickup instructions. Confirm the vehicle, driver, and trip details in the app before getting in, and do not accept a ride from someone who approaches you claiming to be your app driver.

If you cannot access mobile data, use airport Wi-Fi if available or arrange a trusted pickup in advance. Do not share verification codes with another person.

### Taxis and other hired cars

Use a clearly identified official counter or a transport provider you have independently verified. Before travelling, confirm the destination, total fare or pricing method, currency, and whether tolls or other charges are additional. If a price is unclear, ask before accepting the ride.

Avoid unmarked offers from people inside or outside the terminal who pressure you to decide quickly. If you have booked a hotel transfer, confirm the driver's name and meeting point with the hotel before leaving the arrivals area.

### Public transport and onward connections

Public transport options and airport access arrangements can change as infrastructure develops. Do not assume that a metro station, railway connection, or bus service is directly accessible from your arrival terminal or operating at the time you land. Verify current routes, operating hours, station access, and the time needed to reach your departure point before relying on a connection.

If you are travelling onward to another city, allow extra time between landing and any intercity train or bus. Airport immigration, baggage, and road traffic can all cause delays. See our [Bangladesh transport planning guide](/plan-your-trip/transport-in-bangladesh/) if available on the site, and check the relevant operator's current timetable before booking.

For a first stay in Dhaka, review our [Dhaka Travel Guide](/destinations/dhaka-travel-guide/) for neighborhood information and practical arrival planning.

## Step 8: Travel to your hotel safely

Before the car leaves, confirm that the destination shown to the driver matches your hotel or accommodation. Keep your phone charged, share trip details with someone you trust if appropriate, and keep valuables out of sight. If you are arriving late at night, a pre-arranged transfer from your accommodation may be a practical choice.

Choose accommodation with a clear address and contact number, and save those details offline in case your phone has no signal. Our [Bangladesh Travel Safety Guide](/bangladesh-travel-safety/) covers general precautions, while the [Cultural Etiquette Guide](/plan-your-trip/cultural-etiquette/) explains useful local customs for your first days in the country.

## Common questions about arriving at Dhaka airport

### How long does arrival at Dhaka airport take?

There is no fixed time. Immigration queues, whether you need a Visa on Arrival, baggage delivery, customs checks, and the number of flights arriving at the same time can all affect how long you spend at the airport. Avoid scheduling a tight onward connection after an international arrival.

### Can I buy a SIM card at Dhaka airport?

Local SIMs may be available from operator outlets or authorized sellers, but availability and opening hours can vary. Bring the documents required for registration and confirm the price and package terms before purchasing. A compatible eSIM or international roaming may be alternatives.

### Can I use a credit card or withdraw cash at the airport?

Some airport services and ATMs may accept international cards, but acceptance and availability are not guaranteed. Bring a backup payment method and some cash for immediate expenses. Review our [money guide](/plan-your-trip/money-and-currency/) before departure.

### Is it safe to take a taxi or ride-hailing car from Dhaka airport?

Use a verified service, follow official pickup instructions, confirm the vehicle and driver, and agree on the fare or check the app price before travelling. Avoid unsolicited offers and do not get into a vehicle if the details do not match your booking.

### What if my baggage does not arrive?

Report the missing bag to your airline's baggage service desk before leaving the airport. Keep your baggage tag, passport details, and the claim reference, and ask the airline how to track the report and arrange delivery if the bag is found.

### What should I do if my flight arrives late at night?

Confirm your onward transport and accommodation arrangements before departure. Check whether your hotel reception or transfer service will be available when you land, keep the address saved offline, and use verified transport rather than accepting unsolicited offers.

## Dhaka airport arrival checklist

- [ ] Keep your passport, visa, and entry documents in your hand luggage.
- [ ] Save your hotel address and onward travel details offline.
- [ ] Confirm Visa on Arrival eligibility before departure, if applicable.
- [ ] Keep return or onward tickets and evidence of funds available.
- [ ] Collect your baggage and report any problem before leaving the reclaim area.
- [ ] Follow customs rules and declare items or currency when required.
- [ ] Use authorized currency-exchange services and check fees before exchanging money.
- [ ] Arrange a SIM, eSIM, roaming, or other way to access essential travel apps.
- [ ] Verify your transport pickup and destination before entering a vehicle.
- [ ] Allow extra time for traffic and any onward connection.

Airport procedures and service availability can change. Use current airport signage and official instructions on the day of travel, and confirm time-sensitive details before departure.
