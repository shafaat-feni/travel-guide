Planning a trip to Bangladesh starts with confirming which visa or entry permission applies to your passport. **Bangladesh tourist visa requirements depend on your nationality, where you apply, your travel purpose, and the current rules in force.** Do not assume that a visa is available on arrival simply because you hold a Western passport or have visited Bangladesh before.

For the safest plan, confirm the latest requirements with a Bangladesh embassy, high commission, or consulate responsible for your country, and use the official government websites linked below. Visa rules and fees can change, and an airline may refuse boarding if your documents do not meet entry requirements.

## Quick answer: Do you need a visa to visit Bangladesh?

Many international tourists need a visa before travelling to Bangladesh. Some travellers may qualify for a **Visa on Arrival (VoA)**, while others may be eligible for a different arrangement or exemption. Eligibility is not determined by nationality alone in every case; the country from which you travel and whether Bangladesh has a resident diplomatic mission there can also matter.

Use these official resources before booking non-refundable travel:

- <a href="https://visa.gov.bd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bangladesh Online MRV Portal</a> — online forms and visa application guidance.
- <a href="https://voa.specialbranch.gov.bd/public/voa-info.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bangladesh Special Branch: Visa on Arrival informationa</a> — published VoA eligibility categories and requirements.
- <a href="https://dip.gov.bd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bangladesh Department of Immigration and Passports</a> — immigration and passport authority information.
- The Bangladesh diplomatic mission responsible for your place of residence — for advice on your nationality, application location, required documents, and current fees.

If your eligibility is unclear, **apply for an advance visa or obtain written guidance from the relevant Bangladesh mission before departure** rather than relying on being allowed to board or enter.

## 1. Bangladesh tourist visa: advance application

An advance tourist visa is generally the more predictable option when you can apply through a Bangladesh diplomatic mission before travelling. It allows you to address document questions before reaching the airport, although a visa does not remove the need to satisfy immigration requirements on arrival.

### How to apply for a Bangladesh tourist visa

1. **Check the correct application route.** Visit the <a href="https://visa.gov.bd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">official Bangladesh Online MRV Portal</a> and check whether online applications are supported for the country and mission where you intend to apply. The online form is part of the process; it does not necessarily mean the application is completed entirely online.
2. **Complete the application accurately.** Enter your personal details, passport information, travel purpose, and proposed stay exactly as shown in your travel documents. Use an email address you can access.
3. **Print the completed application.** Follow the portal's current instructions. The official portal says applicants should print the completed form and submit it with the required documents to the relevant visa office or Bangladesh mission.
4. **Prepare the supporting documents.** The exact list can vary by mission and applicant. Check the mission's current checklist before attending or sending your application.
5. **Submit the application and passport as instructed.** Follow the mission's appointment, submission, and passport-return procedures. Do not send your passport to an address that you have not verified through an official source.
6. **Pay the applicable fee using the instructed method.** Fees vary by nationality and visa category, and may be affected by reciprocal arrangements. Check the current official fee list and local mission instructions rather than relying on an old fee quoted on a travel website.
7. **Wait for the decision and check the issued visa.** Before booking or departing, confirm the visa's validity dates, permitted entries, and any conditions. Processing times vary, so allow enough time and avoid assuming that a particular date is guaranteed.

### Documents commonly requested for a tourist visa

The definitive checklist is the one issued by the Bangladesh mission handling your application. Depending on your circumstances, you may be asked for:

- A valid passport and copies of relevant passport pages.
- A completed visa application form and photographs that meet the mission's specifications.
- A travel itinerary or proposed arrival and departure dates.
- Return or onward travel details.
- Hotel booking information or the address and details of a host in Bangladesh.
- Evidence of sufficient funds for the planned visit.
- A short explanation of the purpose of travel and planned activities.
- Additional documents requested by the mission for your nationality or circumstances.

These are preparation points, not a guarantee that every item is required in every case. Do not purchase non-refundable flights or accommodation solely because you have submitted an application. Check the mission's instructions about whether reservations must be confirmed and what evidence it accepts.

### Bangladesh tourist visa fees and processing time

There is no single fee or processing time that applies to every foreign tourist. The amount may depend on your passport country, visa type, number of entries, and the relevant reciprocal fee arrangement. The official <a href="https://visa.gov.bd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bangladesh Online MRV Portal</a> publishes a country-based fee table, but you should also confirm the amount and payment method with the mission where you will apply.

Apply well ahead of your intended departure. If your travel date is approaching and you have not received your passport or visa, contact the responsible mission through its official contact details. Avoid relying on unofficial agents who promise guaranteed approval or unusually fast processing.

## 2. Bangladesh Visa on Arrival (VoA): who may qualify?

Bangladesh operates a Visa on Arrival facility for certain categories of travellers at designated points of entry. The official <a href="https://voa.specialbranch.gov.bd/public/voa-info.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bangladesh Special Branch guidance</a> describes eligibility that can depend on nationality, the presence of a Bangladesh embassy or mission in the country concerned, and the purpose and circumstances of travel.

The published categories include certain travellers from countries where Bangladesh has no resident embassy or mission, and nationals of specified countries arriving from a country without a resident Bangladesh embassy or consulate. Other categories may include eligible tourists from European countries, certain foreign nationals of Bangladeshi origin and their family members, and specific official, business, or investment visitors who can provide the required supporting documents.

The official guidance lists the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Russia, China, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, South Korea, several Middle Eastern countries, Turkey, Brunei, and European countries among categories that may qualify under stated conditions. **This list should not be treated as an unconditional guarantee for every passport holder or every itinerary.** Rules and implementation can differ by circumstance and may change.

Before relying on VoA, confirm all of the following with the official guidance or the Bangladesh mission responsible for you:

- Whether your passport nationality is covered.
- Whether your country of departure or transit affects eligibility.
- Whether your travel purpose is permitted under the facility.
- Whether you need to apply or register through the official VoA system before travel.
- Which airport or port you may use to enter and depart.
- Which documents and payment method are currently accepted.

The official Special Branch page states that a VoA may be granted for a **maximum of 30 days as a single-entry visa**. The actual permission granted is subject to the immigration authority's decision at the port of entry.

### Can US, UK, Canadian, Australian, or European tourists get a Bangladesh Visa on Arrival?

Some travellers from these countries may qualify, but you should not rely on nationality alone. Official Bangladesh guidance sets out conditions, including circumstances involving the country from which the traveller arrives and whether Bangladesh has a resident diplomatic mission there. The rules can also distinguish between tourism and other travel purposes.

If you hold a US, UK, Canadian, Australian, New Zealand, or European passport, check the latest <a href="https://voa.specialbranch.gov.bd/public/voa-info.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Special Branch Visa on Arrival rules</a> and ask the relevant Bangladesh mission to confirm your specific case. Obtain an advance visa if required or if you cannot establish that you qualify for VoA.

### Is Visa on Arrival guaranteed?

No. Meeting a published eligibility category does not guarantee admission. The immigration officer reviews the traveller's passport, supporting documents, purpose of visit, and compliance with the applicable rules. A traveller may be asked for additional information or refused a visa or entry.

A Visa on Arrival is therefore not a substitute for checking requirements before departure. If you need certainty before purchasing flights, contact the relevant Bangladesh mission and consider applying for a visa in advance.

## 3. Bangladesh Visa on Arrival requirements and documents

The official Special Branch guidance lists the following core requirements for VoA applicants. Prepare printed copies where practical, and verify whether additional documents apply to your situation.

- **Valid passport:** Carry the passport you will use to enter Bangladesh. Check its remaining validity against your airline's requirements and the instructions issued by the relevant Bangladesh mission; do not assume that one passport-validity rule applies to every traveller.
- **Return or onward ticket:** The official VoA guidance requires a return ticket for a short-term stay. Keep the booking confirmation accessible and make sure your intended departure is consistent with the proposed visit.
- **Evidence of your travel purpose and accommodation:** Carry hotel booking details or the address and contact details of your host, plus an itinerary or invitation documents where relevant. Immigration may ask where you will stay and what you intend to do.
- **Evidence of sufficient funds:** The official Special Branch guidance states that travellers, except those travelling on government duty, should have at least **US$500 in cash or a credit card**. Carry a practical way to demonstrate that you can support yourself during the visit.
- **Visa fee in accepted foreign currency:** The published VoA instructions state that the fee is paid at the point of entry in cash, in US dollars, euros, or pounds sterling. Check the current amount and accepted payment arrangements before flying.
- **Additional documents when applicable:** Business, investment, official, or Bangladeshi-origin family cases may require invitation letters, official clearances, or proof of origin. Tourist applicants may be asked for further supporting information.

Keep these documents together in your hand luggage rather than in checked baggage. ## 4. Bangladesh Visa on Arrival fee: how much should you prepare?

The official Special Branch VoA instructions currently state a standard fee of **US$50, excluding any applicable processing fees**, payable in cash at the designated Sonali Bank PLC counter. The instructions also indicate that fees may be set differently by the authorities, and official diplomatic guidance notes that fee exemptions or reciprocal arrangements can apply in some cases.

Treat US$50 as the published standard reference, not a promise that every traveller will pay exactly that amount. Verify the current fee for your nationality and situation before departure. Bring suitable cash in an accepted currency and do not assume that a bank counter or airport payment desk will accept a credit card.

Do not confuse the VoA fee with the advance tourist visa fee. Advance visa fees vary by nationality and application location; check the <a href="https://visa.gov.bd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">official visa portal's fee information</a> and your local Bangladesh mission.

## 5. Do you need to apply online before arriving for Visa on Arrival?

Bangladesh has an official <a href="https://voa.specialbranch.gov.bd/public/voa-instructions.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Special Branch Visa on Arrival online system</a> that describes account registration, completing traveller information, uploading documents, submitting an application, tracking its status, and downloading an acknowledgement receipt.

The existence of this system does not mean that every traveller has identical pre-arrival steps or that online submission alone guarantees approval. **Check the current official instructions for your eligibility category and follow any application or registration steps that apply to you.** If you submit an online VoA application, keep your tracking details and print the acknowledgement or approval receipt if the system provides one.

Separately, the <a href="https://visa.gov.bd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bangladesh Online MRV Portal</a> supports online forms for several visa-related processes. It instructs applicants for advance visas to print the completed form and submit it with the required documents to the relevant visa office or Bangladesh mission. Do not assume that the advance-visa form and the Special Branch VoA system are interchangeable.

## 6. What happens at Dhaka airport if you use Visa on Arrival?

If your itinerary and eligibility have been confirmed and you arrive at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC), follow the airport signs and current instructions for Visa on Arrival. The official Special Branch instructions describe the general sequence as paying the fee at the designated Sonali Bank PLC counter, collecting the payment receipt, completing the VoA process at the appropriate desk, and then proceeding to immigration.

Allow extra time in your arrival schedule. Queues, document checks, or additional questions can delay the process, and a VoA is not guaranteed. Keep your passport, return ticket, accommodation details, proof of funds, and any required online application receipt easy to access.

## 7. How long can you stay in Bangladesh on a tourist visa or VoA?

The permitted stay depends on the visa issued to you and the conditions recorded on it. The Special Branch guidance describes the Visa on Arrival as a maximum of 30 days, single entry; do not assume that this duration applies to every type of advance tourist visa.

Check the visa sticker or official approval carefully for the permitted stay, validity period, and number of entries. These dates may not mean the same thing: a visa's validity period is not necessarily the number of days you may remain in the country after entry.

If you need to stay longer, contact the <a href="https://dip.gov.bd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Department of Immigration and Passports</a> before your permitted stay expires and ask about the applicable extension process. Do not plan to overstay while waiting for an extension decision. Overstaying can lead to fines, delays, and immigration problems that may affect future travel.

## 8. Can you enter Bangladesh more than once?

Do not assume that a tourist visa or Visa on Arrival permits multiple entries. The official VoA guidance describes the facility as single entry. For an advance visa, check the number of entries printed on the issued visa and confirm how it applies to your itinerary.

If you plan to leave Bangladesh during your trip and return later, clarify the correct visa type before travelling. This is especially important for itineraries that combine Bangladesh with nearby countries. A single-entry visa may no longer be usable after you leave Bangladesh, even if its original validity dates have not passed.

## 9. Passport, transit, and airline checks before departure

Before travelling, check your documents against the requirements of Bangladesh immigration, your airline, and any country where you will transit. Transit requirements can differ from destination-entry rules, particularly if you change airports, pass through border control, or travel on separate tickets.

Use this checklist:

- Confirm the visa or exemption required for your exact passport and itinerary.
- Check passport validity and blank-page requirements with the relevant Bangladesh mission and airline.
- Make sure your name and passport details match across your application, ticket, and other documents.
- Keep your return or onward ticket and accommodation details accessible.
- Carry evidence of funds and the correct visa fee if you intend to apply for VoA.
- Print your visa, application receipt, or approval documents where applicable, and keep secure digital copies as backup.
- Recheck official requirements shortly before departure in case of a policy change.

## 10. Common Bangladesh visa mistakes to avoid

**Assuming every Western passport qualifies for VoA.** Eligibility can depend on more than nationality. Confirm the rule for your specific circumstances before travelling.

**Treating online application as final approval.** Completing a form or receiving an acknowledgement is not necessarily the same as having a visa issued or being approved for entry.

**Using outdated fee information.** Visa fees and payment arrangements can change or differ by nationality. Check the official portal and the responsible Bangladesh mission.

**Arriving without supporting documents.** Even when eligible for VoA, you may need to demonstrate the purpose of your trip, accommodation arrangements, return travel, and sufficient funds.

**Booking an itinerary that requires multiple entries on a single-entry visa.** Confirm the number of entries before finalising travel between Bangladesh and other countries.

**Relying on unofficial websites or agents.** Use government portals and official diplomatic missions for rules, fees, and application instructions. Never submit false information or forged documents.

**Waiting until the last minute.** Advance applications, passport return, or additional document requests can take time. Start early and keep a margin before departure.

Before you leave for the airport, make sure you have:

- [ ] Confirmed the visa requirement for your nationality and itinerary.
- [ ] Obtained an advance visa, or verified that you meet the current Visa on Arrival conditions.
- [ ] Checked the current fee and payment method through official sources.
- [ ] Prepared your passport, return or onward ticket, accommodation details, and evidence of sufficient funds.
