Navigating visa and entry requirements is the critical first step in planning a trip to Bangladesh. Depending on your nationality, purpose of travel, and diplomatic presence in your country of residence, you may obtain an advance tourist visa through an official embassy or apply for a Visa on Arrival (VoA) upon landing at major international entry points.

## Advance Tourist Visa (Online Application)

For most foreign nationals, applying for an advance tourist visa via the official Bangladesh Online Visa Portal is the recommended and standard approach.

### Application Steps
1. **Complete the Online Application:** Fill out the required personal, passport, and travel details on the official [Bangladesh Online Visa Portal](https://www.visa.gov.bd/).
2. **Submit Passport & Documentation:** Print the completed application summary and submit it along with your physical passport, passport-sized photographs, flight itinerary, and hotel booking confirmation to your nearest Bangladesh diplomatic mission or embassy.
3. **Pay Visa Fees:** Pay the prescribed visa processing fee determined by your country's reciprocal fee structure.
4. **Visa Issuance:** Once processed, collect your passport containing the stamped visa before departure.

## Visa on Arrival (VoA) Eligibility & Rules

Bangladesh provides a Visa on Arrival facility at [Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC)](/plan-your-trip/dhaka-airport-arrival-guide/) in Dhaka and selected international ports under specific conditions.

### Eligible Categories & Conditions
* **Nationals Without Local Missions:** Passport holders from countries where Bangladesh does not maintain a resident diplomatic mission are generally eligible for VoA consideration.
* **Tourists from Specific Jurisdictions:** Passport holders from the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Russia, and Schengen European nations traveling for tourism may be considered for a 30-day single-entry Visa on Arrival.
* **Immigration Discretion:** Visa on Arrival is not an automatic right; final entry approval rests entirely with the duty immigration officer at the port of entry.

### Mandatory VoA Requirements & Documents
To apply for a Visa on Arrival at the airport, visitors should present:
* A valid passport with at least 6 months of remaining validity from the date of entry and at least two blank visa pages.
* A confirmed return or onward flight ticket departing Bangladesh within 30 days.
* Proof of hotel reservation or verified host accommodation address in Bangladesh.
* Proof of sufficient financial means (minimum USD 500 in cash or an international credit card; see our [Money and Currency Guide](/plan-your-trip/money-and-currency/) for payment details).
* Payment in USD cash for the Visa on Arrival fee (standard baseline fee is USD 50 plus minor local bank counter processing fees, payable at the Sonali Bank PLC counter in the arrival hall).

> **Important Advisory:** Completing the pre-registration form on `visa.gov.bd` prior to flying generates an application summary that speeds up airport handling. Whenever feasible, obtaining an advance visa from a Bangladeshi embassy prior to travel eliminates airport delays and boarding risks. For safety advisories, consult our [Safety and Security Guide](/bangladesh-travel-safety/).

## Essential Pre-Departure Checklist
* Confirm that your passport has at least two blank visa pages and 6 months validity.
* Carry printed physical copies of hotel bookings, onward tickets, and return itineraries.
* Maintain cash in US Dollars for airport fees, as card processing terminals at airport bank desks may experience network interruptions. Review our [Dhaka Airport Arrival Guide](/plan-your-trip/dhaka-airport-arrival-guide/) for terminal steps.
